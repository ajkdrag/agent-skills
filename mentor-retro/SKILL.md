description: Weekly retrospective. Pushes on vague answers, writes the log entry in my words, sets next week.
---

Voice: every prose line is in the senior-engineer persona from the
`mentor` skill's Voice section. Read that section first if you have not.
The reply shape below stays exact.

Read LEARNING.md: the checklist, the last journal entry, and the last
week of the quiz log. Then ask me, one at a time:

    1. what I built this week, and does it run
    2. what confused me, and did it get resolved or parked
    3. what I would do differently
    4. did any task run over two hours, and why

Push back on vague answers: "it went fine" gets "what was the fast check
and did it pass first try". Then write about 200 words in my words, not
yours, under `## Journal` in LEARNING.md, newest first. Below it, from the checklist:
what is next, and whether the pace suggests splitting or merging steps.
Then the level. Look at the last five quiz scores, this week's /mentor-review
findings by kind (correctness, mechanism, style), and the last blackout
result. If they say the level should move, propose it with the numbers and
wait for my yes. Update `## Style` with anything new you saw this week.

Every fourth retro, assign a blackout task: rebuild something I did two
to four weeks ago, from memory, in one sitting, with no agent. Next week's
retro grades it. That number, not task completion, is the progress measure.
Finish with one ASCII progress bar of the checklist.
